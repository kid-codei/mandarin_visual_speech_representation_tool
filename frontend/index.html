Hi! My name is John Henry Cruz and this project is the Mandarin Visual Speech Representation Tool (MVSRT).

This tool helps Mandarin learners visualize and compare their pitch contour against native speakers — providing character-level, real-time feedback to support clearer, more natural pronunciation.

Whether you're practicing tones, preparing for exams, or simply improving your Mandarin, I hope this tool makes your learning journey easier, more intuitive, and more fun.
