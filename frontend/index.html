Mandarin Visual Speech Representation Tool

Real-time pitch contour comparison for Mandarin tone learning.

MVSRT helps Mandarin learners visualize and compare their pitch contour against native speaker recordings — providing character-level, real-time feedback to support clearer, more natural pronunciation of tonal distinctions.

  • Interactive pitch contour plots with native vs. learner overlay
  • Character-level tone scoring with coaching hints (Normal, Lenient, Strict)
  • MFA (Montreal Forced Aligner) integration for accurate segment alignment
  • Traditional, Simplified, and Pinyin display modes
  • DTW-based learner pitch alignment and persistent attempt history
